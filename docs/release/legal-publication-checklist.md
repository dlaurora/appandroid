# Legal Publication Checklist

- [ ] Privacy policy reflects actual app behavior.
- [ ] Terms of use reflect actual app behavior and limits.
- [ ] Service/support policy reflects actual support, local-first behavior, and data recovery limits.
- [ ] Data Safety draft matches app code and dependencies.
- [ ] Privacy contact and support email are final and present in publishable docs.
- [ ] Responsible developer or legal entity is final and present in publishable docs.
- [ ] Jurisdiction/applicable-law decision is final where required.
- [ ] Public privacy policy URL exists.
- [ ] In-app privacy policy is available offline.
- [ ] In-app terms of use are available offline.
- [ ] In-app service/support policy is available offline if referenced by the app or store listing.
- [ ] Data retention and deletion policy matches implemented archive/deactivate/delete behavior.
- [ ] Policies do not claim encryption, backup, sync, permanent deletion, uptime, support levels, or compliance that is not implemented.
- [ ] Legal disclaimers do not claim compliance or guarantees that are not implemented.
- [ ] Google Play Data Safety answers are consistent with privacy policy, in-app disclosures, permissions, and third-party SDK behavior.
- [ ] Professional legal review completed before commercial publication.
