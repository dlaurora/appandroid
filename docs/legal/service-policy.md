# Service And Support Policy

## Status

Publication-oriented draft aligned with the current local-first TechQuote MVP. This is not legal advice and requires professional review before commercial publication.

Release blockers before publication:

- final support contact;
- final developer or responsible legal entity;
- final support availability decision;
- final effective date.

## Service Model

TechQuote is currently designed as an offline-first Android app. Implemented data is stored locally on the user's device. The current MVP does not provide cloud accounts, cloud sync, managed backup, server-side storage, remote administration, analytics, advertising, or external integrations.

## Supported Use

Current implemented support scope:

- app installation and launch guidance;
- local client management guidance;
- local data behavior explanation;
- local service catalog guidance;
- local product/spare-part catalog guidance;
- explanation of local prices, quantities, SKU, categories, activation, and deactivation behavior;
- troubleshooting build or app behavior during development.

## Support Limits

TechQuote support does not provide:

- emergency service;
- 24/7 availability unless a later business decision explicitly creates it;
- legal, tax, accounting, fiscal, or professional certification advice;
- guarantee that user-entered prices, catalog items, quotes, or reports are accurate;
- recovery of data from a lost, reset, damaged, cleared, or uninstalled device when backup/import is not implemented;
- guarantee of compatibility with every Android device, manufacturer modification, or unsupported OS version;
- support for fraudulent, unlawful, or unauthorized use.

## Data Recovery

Backup/import is not implemented in the current MVP. Users should treat local device data as at risk until an approved backup/import feature exists. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove TechQuote data from the device.

## Updates

App updates may change features, screens, supported Android versions, and local data schemas. Database migrations must be tested before release. Release notes should describe material changes.

## Security Contact

A final security or support contact must be added before commercial publication. Security reports should not include unnecessary personal data, client data, prices, SKU, photos, PDFs, or database files unless explicitly requested through a safe support process.

## Payments, Licenses, And Refunds

The current MVP does not implement in-app purchases, subscriptions, license checks, or paid service plans. Payment, licensing, cancellation, and refund terms must be added before monetization is released.
